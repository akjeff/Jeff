The Proposal

One question.
One evening.

An intimate, cinematic glasshouse proposal inspired by I See the Light — warm lanterns overhead, candlelight below, white flowers, deep evening tones, and the people Emma loves waiting just out of sight.

The setup

This is the visual target.

Dark glasshouse. Warm lanterns suspended overhead. White florals concentrated around the proposal point. Candlelight lining the floor. Elegant, intimate and cinematic — not overdecorated.

Reference direction: lantern canopy + warm candles + white florals + dark evening backdrop.
40+ lanterns

The ceiling is the hero.

Varied heights, warm glow, no harsh white LEDs.

30 candles

Ground-level glow.

Clear cylinders and tall tapers to build depth without clutter.

2 + 4 florals

Flowers, not foliage.

Two statement white arrangements plus four smaller accents. Keep greenery restrained.

The palette

Black. Glass. Cream. White. Gold. Amber light.

No balloons. No neon sign. No fake trees. No bright colours.

The moment
