The Proposal

One question.
One evening.

An intimate, cinematic glasshouse proposal inspired by I See the Light — warm lanterns overhead, candlelight below, white flowers, deep evening tones, and the people Emma loves waiting just out of sight.

Scroll to enter the plan ↓
Visual language

Luxury through restraint.

40+ lanterns

The ceiling is the hero.

Warm paper lanterns suspended at varied heights. Dense enough to feel magical, controlled enough to photograph beautifully.

30 candles

Light at ground level.

Clear cylinders, varied heights, symmetrical aisle placement. No clutter.

2 + 4 florals

White, not green.

Two statement arrangements at the proposal point, four smaller accents.

The palette

Black. Glass. Cream. White. Gold. Amber light.

No balloons. No neon sign. No fake trees. No coloured flower mix.

The moment

Don't rush the question.

She enters. She looks up. She sees the lanterns. She sees you. Let the moment breathe.

0:00

Emma enters.

Music low. Photographer hidden. No family in view.

0:30

Meet her.

Take her hands. Give her time to absorb the room.

1:00

Past → Her → Future.

A 60–90 second speech. Personal, specific, calm.

2:00

The question.

“Emmanuella, will you marry me?”

+5 min

Just you two.

Photos continue quietly. No immediate crowd rush.

+10 min

Family reveal.

Friends and family enter. Drinks, hugs, dessert and photos.
